## How to verify each link is publicly viewable

1. **Open it in a private / incognito window** where you are not signed in to Google, GitHub, etc. If you see the content without a login prompt, it's public.
2. **Google Drive / Docs / Slides:** Share → General access → *Anyone with the link* → *Viewer*. Re-test in incognito.
3. **YouTube:** Visibility *Unlisted* or *Public* (not Private). Confirm playback in incognito.
4. **Loom:** Share settings → *Anyone with the link can view*.
5. **GitHub:** Repository Settings → General → Danger Zone → visibility *Public*. Open the repo URL in incognito.
6. **Deployed app:** Open in incognito and on mobile data (not your Wi-Fi). Open `/?ref=AISPRINT-XX000` and confirm the page loads (an unknown code should show a "not recognised" note on the Register page).
7. **Ask a friend** to open every link from their own device and confirm.
