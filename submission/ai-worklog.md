## Entry 1: Asset selection

**Prompt:** What is an effective asset to promote a student AI workshop?

**AI suggestion:** Build a conventional landing page.

**What I changed or rejected:** Rejected a plain landing page as the primary asset because it gives students no value before registering.

**Final decision:** Explore an interactive project generator and referral-sharing flow to give students value and encourage organic sharing.

**Learning:** An asset that delivers value up front can double as the reason to share.

## Entry 2: Channel selection

**Prompt:** How can a campaign aim for 500 registrations with ₹2,000?

**AI suggestion:** Spread the campaign across paid advertising, influencers, email, and social media.

**What I changed or rejected:** Rejected spreading ₹2,000 across many paid channels — the budget is too small for paid reach to matter.

**Final decision:** Prioritise college communities and referrals while keeping paid promotion limited and measurable.

**Learning:** With a tiny budget, trust-based distribution matters more than paid reach.

---

## Entry 3: Technical scope

**Prompt:** How can I automate referral tracking and campaign reporting?

**AI suggestion:** Build a complex backend with several integrations.

**What I changed or rejected:** Deferred the backend; the challenge needs a working demonstration, not production infrastructure.

**Final decision:** Start with a functional local prototype and add production infrastructure only when necessary.

**Learning:** Scope to what proves the idea; document what production would need.
